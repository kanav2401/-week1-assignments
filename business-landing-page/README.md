# EstateVista – Real Estate Business Landing Page

## Project Overview

EstateVista is a modern and responsive real estate business landing page developed as part of the WeIntern Web Development Internship – Week 1 Assignment.

The website is designed to present property listings, real estate services, and contact information for customers interested in buying, selling, or renting properties in Mohali, Chandigarh, Panchkula, Zirakpur, and nearby areas.

The project focuses on professional branding, property discovery, responsive layouts, and user-friendly navigation.
